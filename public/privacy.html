Privacy Policy

Tube Tangle — last updated 11 September 2026

This is a single-player puzzle game. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. It does not ask for your name, your email address, your location or anything else that identifies you, and there is nothing in it that could.

What is stored, and where

The game keeps your progress in your own browser, using localStorage on the device you are playing on. That is the whole of it:

  • which level you are on, and the highest one you have unlocked
  • stars earned and fewest moves taken, per level
  • your coin balance and the boosters you own
  • your sound, music and alert settings
  • a few bookkeeping values the difficulty and reward systems use
  • a random id, described under The event log below

Every one of these keys begins with bsp_, and none of them describes you — only how far you have got. Clearing your browser's site data removes them permanently.

The event log

So that we can tell which levels are too hard, the game records what happens to a level and sends it to a database we run. A row is written when a level starts, is finished, is restarted, is skipped, when a booster is used, and when a board reaches a dead end. Each row holds:

  • the level, the puzzle it dealt, and how hard that puzzle is on paper — its shortest solution, its number of colours and tubes
  • what happened — moves taken, stars, how long it took, which boosters were spent and what they cost
  • a random id for the browser, and a random id for the sitting

The browser id is generated from the clock and a random number the first time it is needed. It is not derived from anything about you or your device, it is never shown to anyone, and it exists only so that two rows can be known to have come from the same browser. Clearing your browser's site data throws it away and a new one is made, with no way to connect the two.

A row contains no name, email address, IP-derived location, advertising identifier or device fingerprint. Nothing in the log can be traced back to a person, by us or by anyone else.

The database is Firebase Realtime Database, run by Google on our behalf, with its data held in Singapore. We do not sell, share or transfer what is in it.

When the game is played on CrazyGames

On CrazyGames the game runs inside their page and uses their SDK for two things: to save the progress listed above to your CrazyGames account so it follows you between devices, and to respect the mute button on their page.

In that arrangement CrazyGames processes that data on our behalf, and their own privacy policy — crazygames.com/privacy — covers what they collect as the host of the page, including any advertising they serve around the game. We do not receive, see or store any of it.

What the game does not do

  • No tracking of you across other sites or apps
  • No profile built about you, and no profiling of any kind
  • No advertising SDK of our own
  • No cookies
  • No data sold, shared or transferred to anyone
  • No purchases, and no payment details

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anybody, it collects none from children either. The event log described above holds no personal information about a player of any age.

Changes

If the game ever starts collecting something this page does not describe, this page changes on the same day. The date at the top is when it was last true.
