Privacy Policy

Ball Sort Puzzle · Color Game — last updated 10 September 2026

This is a single-player puzzle game. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. It does not ask for your name, your email address, your location or anything else that identifies you, and there is nothing in it that could.

What is stored, and where

The game keeps your progress in your own browser, using localStorage on the device you are playing on. That is the whole of it:

  • which level you are on, and the highest one you have unlocked
  • stars earned and fewest moves taken, per level
  • your coin balance and the boosters you own
  • your sound, music and alert settings
  • a few bookkeeping values the difficulty and reward systems use

Every one of these keys begins with bsp_. None of them is sent anywhere by the game, and none of them describes you — only how far you have got. Clearing your browser's site data, or using Erase all progress in the game's settings, removes them permanently.

When the game is played on CrazyGames

On CrazyGames the game runs inside their page and uses their SDK for two things: to save the progress listed above to your CrazyGames account so it follows you between devices, and to respect the mute button on their page.

In that arrangement CrazyGames processes that data on our behalf, and their own privacy policy — crazygames.com/privacy — covers what they collect as the host of the page, including any advertising they serve around the game. We do not receive, see or store any of it.

What the game does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry or tracking of any kind
  • No advertising SDK of our own
  • No cookies
  • No data sold, shared or transferred to anyone
  • No purchases, and no payment details

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anybody, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If the game ever starts collecting something this page does not describe, this page changes on the same day. The date at the top is when it was last true.
